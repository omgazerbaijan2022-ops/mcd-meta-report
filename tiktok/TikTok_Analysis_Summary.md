# McDonald's Azerbaijan: TikTok Ads analysis summary

**BEFORE:** 1 Apr – 30 Jun 2026 (previous agency) · **AFTER:** 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 (current agency)

**Source:** two TikTok Ads Manager ad-level exports (USD).
- Zero-spend legacy rows are excluded: 54 BEFORE and 83 AFTER. That leaves 47 and 51 active ads.
- Active-row sums match each export's Total row exactly for spend, impressions, clicks and 2-second views.
- All rates are recalculated from summed totals.

## Key findings

1. **Reach efficiency is the clearest win, and it isn't just a budget effect.** AFTER spent 10% less but reached 14% more unique people. Cost per 1,000 people reached fell 21% ($5.53 → $4.36) while CPM barely moved (+1.6%). The gain came from spreading impressions across more people: frequency fell from 21.4 to 16.6 impressions per person.

2. **Clicks fell faster than budget.** Clicks fell 17% on 10% less spend, CTR dropped from 0.234% to 0.219%, and CPC rose from $0.111 to $0.120. Six Q3 ads, with $1.9K (7.8% of spend), recorded 0–1 clicks:
   - three Spiderman launch ads;
   - the September Generic video;
   - the Nizami Mall opening ($622, 1 click);
   - an iOS app-promo test ($0.02).

   The export does not show whether these ads had a click destination.

3. **Video views are the weakest area, and the drop is concentrated in three ads.** 2-second views fell 30%, the view rate fell from 22.9% to 18.1%, and cost per 1,000 views rose 29%. The three largest Q3 Reach ads were:
   - McChicken value (*Xususi toyuq kombolari_03.06*);
   - McCafé *Cofe_#1*;
   - McCafé *Cofe_#2*.

   Together they took 26% of spend ($6.3K) at a 7.0% view rate and frequency 2.7–3.3. All other Q3 ads held a 21.7% view rate, close to Q2's 22.9%.

4. **The new Video views campaigns are the most efficient video source.** They are new in Q3 and took $2.1K (9% of spend). They delivered 2.7M 2-second views at $0.78 per 1,000 views, with a 41% view rate. Q3 Reach campaigns cost $1.62 per 1,000 views, and Q2 Reach campaigns $1.08.

5. **Frequency drives the cost per person reached.** Across Q3 Reach ads with at least $200 spend, ad-level frequency and cost per 1,000 reached are strongly correlated (r = 0.75; Q2 r = 0.65). This is an association, not proof of cause. The worst campaigns were:
   - McChicken value (Jul): $1.07 per 1,000, one ad, $3.6K, frequency 3.3;
   - McCafé (Sep): $0.63 per 1,000, frequency 2.7.

   The best were Cheddar Sauce ($0.20), Grimace ($0.23) and the Mixology launch ($0.28), all at frequency around 1.1.

6. **Like-for-like results are mixed** (Reach-objective ads, matched by product keyword):

   | Initiative | Cost per 1,000 reached | Note |
   |---|---:|---|
   | Grimace | −27% | |
   | Mixology | −28% | only 1 Q2 ad |
   | FIFA | −2% | view rate +12% |
   | Happy Meal | +9% | only 1 Q2 ad |
   | Chicken | +69% | |
   | McCafé | +154% | view rate −78% |

   CTR improved in four of the six initiatives.

7. **Ad group findings (Q3 only; Q2 had one "all" ad group per campaign):**
   - **iOS vs Android:** the same creative, *Chicken_offer_inf_16.08.26*, cost $0.78 per 1,000 reached on iOS vs $0.31 on Android (2.5×). CPM was $0.50 vs $0.28.
   - **Happy Meal audiences:** the "GenZ (with GenA interest)" ad groups out-clicked the Parents / main Happy Meal ad groups in both months: 0.29% vs 0.19% CTR in August, 0.43% vs 0.29% in September.

8. **Creative leaders and laggards** (ads with ≥1M impressions):
   - **CTR:** *red_card_boost_01.06* (FIFA, 0.34%), *MCFlurry_IMG_6519* (0.32%), *Futbol_əyləncə_və_dadlı_anlar* (0.32%), *Chicken_offer_inf_16.08.26* on Android (0.30%).
   - **View rate:** *MCFlurry_IMG_6519* (53.5%) and *Generic_1* (51.8%), both from Video views campaigns.
   - **Influencer-named offers:** the three ads with "inf" in the name averaged a 35.0% view rate.
   - **Weakest:** the three high-budget, low-view Reach ads above, and the zero-click launch ads.

9. **Account structure changed.**
   - **BEFORE:** 6 campaigns (monthly Reach buckets plus community interaction), each with one ad group named "all" holding up to 24 ads.
   - **AFTER:** 20 product-led campaigns with 24 ad groups named by audience or OS, plus new Video views and app-promotion tests.
   - **Cut-over:** clean. No previous-agency ad spent in July–September.

## Data caveats

- **Results:** the "Results" column mixes unnamed result types (reach, community interactions, video-view results, one app result), so results are not compared across objectives.
- **Not in the exports:** 6-second views, likes, comments, shares, engagement rate, conversions, a time breakdown and a placement split. Engagement cannot be assessed.
- **Objectives** are inferred from campaign names and results. Opening_Nizami Mall reports results that differ from its reach, so it is shown as "objective not labelled".
- **Reach below account level:** campaign, ad group and ad comparisons use ad-level reach, which counts a person once per ad. Only the account total is de-duplicated.
