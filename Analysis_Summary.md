# McDonald's Azerbaijan: Meta Ads analysis summary

**BEFORE:** 1 Apr – 30 Jun 2026 (previous agency) · **AFTER:** 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 (current agency)
Source: two Meta Ads Manager exports, one row per ad (77 ads BEFORE, 106 ads AFTER). All rates are recalculated from summed totals (weighted), never averaged across rows.

## Headline KPIs

| KPI | BEFORE | AFTER | Change | Read |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Amount spent | $37,801 | $50,304 | +33.1% | Investment |
| Impressions | 84.28M | 87.64M | +4.0% | Volume |
| Reach (sum of ad-level reach)¹ | 48.07M | 62.41M | +29.8% | Volume |
| Frequency (impr ÷ reach)¹ | 1.75 | 1.40 | −19.9% | Context (wider spread) |
| CPM | $0.449 | $0.574 | +28.0% | **Worse** |
| Cost per 1,000 reached | $0.786 | $0.806 | +2.5% | Slightly worse |
| Link clicks | 61,430 | 94,440 | +53.7% | Volume |
| Link CTR | 0.073% | 0.108% | +47.8% | **Better** |
| CTR (all)² | 0.180% | 0.184% | +2.5% | Better |
| CPC (link) | $0.615 | $0.533 | −13.4% | **Better** |
| Post engagements | 9.50M | 9.35M | −1.6% | Volume |
| Cost per post engagement | $0.0040 | $0.0054 | +35.2% | **Worse** |
| 3-second video plays | 9.32M | 9.10M | −2.3% | Volume |
| Cost per 1,000 3s plays | $4.06 | $5.53 | +36.2% | **Worse** |

¹ Meta does not de-duplicate reach across ads in an ad-level export, so summed reach overstates unique people. The method is the same in both periods.
² The export has no "clicks (all)" column, so all clicks were rebuilt as CTR(all) × impressions for each ad.

## Key findings

1. **Budget scale-up and reach grew together.** Spend rose 33% ($37.8K → $50.3K) and summed reach rose 30%. Impressions grew only 4%, so the new setup spread delivery across more people: frequency fell from 1.75 to 1.40. This is mostly **scale**, not efficiency.

2. **Traffic is the clearest efficiency gain.** Link clicks rose 54%, faster than budget. Link CTR rose 48% and CPC fell 13% ($0.62 → $0.53). The shift came from Reach campaigns, whose link clicks rose from 15.4K to 75.2K (4.9×). In Q2, most Reach ads recorded 0–2 link clicks.

3. **Reach campaigns now reach people more cheaply, despite higher CPM.** In Reach-objective campaigns (84% of BEFORE spend, 76% of AFTER spend):
   - cost per 1,000 people reached fell 5% ($0.71 → $0.67), while CPM rose 23% ($0.40 → $0.50) and frequency fell from 1.76 to 1.35;
   - reach results rose 26% on 20% more spend;
   - leaving out two September campaigns (Entry Level, Happy Meal Sep), Q3 Reach cost per 1,000 reached was $0.58 (−18% vs Q2).

4. **Media cost is the main headwind.** Account CPM rose 28%, so each dollar bought 22% fewer impressions (2,230 → 1,742). The rise shows up inside the Reach objective too, so it is not only a change in objective mix.

5. **Engagement and video stayed flat on a bigger budget.** 97–98% of post engagements are 3-second video plays, so "engagement" here mostly means video consumption.
   - Post engagements fell 2% and 3-second plays fell 2%.
   - Cost per engagement rose 35%, and cost per 1,000 3s plays rose 36%.
   - Video ads hooked viewers slightly better (3s-play rate 13.3% → 14.1%), but video's share of spend fell from 82% to 75%, and CPM rose.

6. **About 26% of AFTER spend ($13.1K) sits in three under-performing pockets:**
   - **EntryLevel_Reach_Sep_26** ($4.3K): $1.21 per 1,000 reached, frequency 2.3, 0.12% engagement rate.
   - **HM_Reach_Sep'26** ($4.2K): $1.88 per 1,000 reached, frequency 2.6.
   - **Interactions-optimised engagement ads** ($4.6K, new in Q3): $0.0164 per post engagement, 6.2× the $0.0026 achieved with the Post-engagement goal.

7. **Audience fatigue shows at ad-set level.** The same Happy Meal audiences cost 2.3–3.8× more to reach in September than in August:
   - Gen A interest-based: $0.59 → $2.25 per 1,000 reached, as frequency rose from 1.32 to 3.14.
   - Parents: $0.69 → $1.58, as frequency rose from 1.06 to 2.19.

   The Happy Meal look-alike traffic ad set ran at frequency 6.2 and $1.95 CPM.

8. **Like-for-like results are mixed.** Cost per 1,000 reached, comparing only initiatives that appear in both periods (Reach-objective ads):

   | Initiative | Change |
   |---|---:|
   | Grimace | −38% |
   | Restaurant openings | −18% |
   | Mixology | −17% |
   | FIFA / World Cup | −13% |
   | Chicken | +32% |
   | McCafé | +43% |

   Q3's Chicken and McCafé ads did earn link clicks (CTR ≈ 0.09%), which their Q2 versions did not.

9. **Creative patterns:**
   - Spiderman GenZ menu creatives led on clicks (0.32–0.55% link CTR, $0.14–0.21 CPC).
   - Mixology (MCxCola content 5, 43.8% engagement rate) and FIFA videos (43.1%) led on attention.
   - Creator/influencer content earned about 3× the engagement rate of other Reach ads in **both** periods: Q2 25.3% vs 9.7%, Q3 24.6% vs 7.9%.
   - The weakest ads were static reach posts with near-zero response, e.g. Entry_Level_#1 ($2.8K, 0.06% engagement).

10. **Account structure changed.** The account went from 15 campaigns organised by objective and month, with 77 single-ad ad sets named after creatives, to 31 product-led campaigns with 58 audience-named ad sets (1.8 ads per ad set). New goals were added: link clicks, interactions, 2-second video views, and an app-install test.

## Data caveats

- Result types differ between periods and objectives, so they are never summed. For example, Q2 Traffic campaigns optimised for Instagram profile visits (60,443 visits at $0.053 each), while Q3 Traffic optimised for link clicks (11,314 clicks at $0.254 each).
- **AFTER** includes 6 carry-over ads from the previous agency's Reach_June'26 campaign ($1,119, 2.2% of spend). **BEFORE** includes 2 March-named ads that delivered in April ($1,108).
- The 3 app-install ads (AFTER, $95) report no results.
- One AFTER ad name appears twice in the same ad set with different delivery; it is treated as two ads.
- Blank link clicks and 3s plays are treated as zero.
- The exports have no daily or monthly breakdown (so no trend lines), no placement split and no sales or conversion data.
- Campaign names were not assumed to be equivalent. Initiatives were matched only by product keywords that appear in both periods.

Full calculation tables are in `analysis/analysis_tables.xlsx`.
